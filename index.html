We study how liquids move, deform, and break apart.

GabLab is an experimental fluid mechanics group in the George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech, led by Chase T. Gabbard. We focus on interfacial flow in multiphase and complex media: drops that bounce off liquid baths, spheres that plunge into water, films that bead up as they flow down fibers, and particle-laden interfaces that buckle and break.

Our mission is to uncover the fundamental mechanisms behind these flows by pairing careful experiments and high-speed imaging with rigorous analysis. What we learn informs industrial processes such as heat and mass transfer, explains phenomena seen in nature, and guides the design of systems that control fluid motion.
