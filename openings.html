PhD positions in experimental fluid mechanics

Positions
Multiple PhD students
Start date
Fall 2027
Location
Georgia Tech, Atlanta

GabLab is recruiting multiple PhD students to begin in Fall 2027 in the George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech. Students in the lab design and build their own experiments, capture fluid phenomena with high-speed imaging, and connect what they see to theory.

Projects span our research themes of interfacial phenomena, complex fluids, granular matter, and fluid-structure interactions, including drop impact and rebound, water entry, liquid sheets, and particle-laden interfaces. See our research for current projects.

Who should apply

We welcome applicants with a background in mechanical engineering, physics, chemical engineering, applied mathematics, or a related field. Experience with experiments, programming, or fluid mechanics is helpful, but curiosity and persistence matter most.

How to apply

  1. Apply to the PhD program through Georgia Tech Mechanical Engineering graduate admissions, and mention GabLab in your application.
  2. Email Chase Gabbard at chase.gabbard@me.gatech.edu with your CV and a few sentences about the research you’d like to do.
