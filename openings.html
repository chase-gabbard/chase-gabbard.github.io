Fall 2027

The Gabbard Lab is recruiting multiple PhD students to begin in Fall 2027 in the George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech. Lab members will design and build novel experiments to investigate fluid phenomena, and couple their observations with theory and numerical predictions.

How to apply

  1. Explore our research

    Look through our current and past research and publications to make sure the Gabbard Lab aligns with your interests.

  2. Email the PI

    Send your CV, a few sentences about the research you would like to do, and the email addresses of two professional references to chase.gabbard@me.gatech.edu.

  3. Apply to Georgia Tech

    Submit a PhD application to the George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering by December 15, 2026, and mention the Gabbard Lab in your application.
