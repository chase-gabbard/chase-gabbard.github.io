Fully funded PhD positions in experimental fluid mechanics

Fall 2027

The Gabbard Lab is recruiting multiple fully funded PhD students to begin in Fall 2027 in the George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering at Georgia Tech. Students in the lab design and build their own experiments, capture fluid phenomena with high-speed imaging, and connect what they see to theory.

Areas of interest

New students will help launch projects in areas such as:

  • Formation and transport of complex drops. How complex-fluid drops form from jets and sprays, then spread, rebound, and travel on surfaces, from spray cooling to agriculture.
  • Dynamic wetting transitions on subfreezing surfaces. How drops spread, stick, or rebound on surfaces below freezing, where wetting races solidification, to guide anti-icing designs.
  • Particle capture at complex and soft interfaces. How particles are captured, rebound, or escape at drops, films, and soft interfaces, from raindrops scavenging pollutants to wet scrubbers.

Who should apply

We welcome applicants who are excited to build experiments and explore new physics. Ideal candidates have:

  • A B.S. or M.S. in mechanical, aerospace, or chemical engineering, physics, applied math, or a related field
  • Curiosity about fluid mechanics, soft matter, and the physics of everyday phenomena
  • Enthusiasm for hands-on work: building setups, machining, 3D printing, or electronics
  • Some programming for data and image analysis (e.g., Python or MATLAB), or eagerness to learn
  • Clear communication, teamwork, and persistence

Prior research experience is helpful, but curiosity and persistence matter most.

How to apply

  1. Explore our research. Look through our current and past research and publications to make sure the Gabbard Lab aligns with your interests.
  2. Email Chase. Send your CV, a few sentences about the research you would like to do, and the email addresses of two professional references to chase.gabbard@me.gatech.edu.
  3. Apply to Georgia Tech. Submit a PhD application to the George W. Woodruff School of Mechanical Engineering by December 15, 2026, and mention the Gabbard Lab in your application.
