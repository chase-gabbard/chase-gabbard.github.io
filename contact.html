In the heart of Atlanta

Georgia Tech’s campus sits in Midtown Atlanta, at the center of one of the Southeast’s largest hubs for research, technology, and industry. The lab is minutes from Tech Square, where startups and corporate innovation centers work alongside university researchers.

Beyond campus, Atlanta offers lively neighborhoods, a thriving food and music scene, and green spaces like the BeltLine and Piedmont Park. Hartsfield–Jackson International Airport makes it easy for collaborators from around the world to visit.
